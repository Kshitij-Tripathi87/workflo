# Cortex Autopilot — Target Account List

> 100 candidate accounts for the founder-led customer discovery sprint.
> Update weekly. Don't reach out to the same contact twice.

## Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

| Dimension | Threshold |
|-----------|-----------|
| **Company size** | 50–1,000 employees (data team of 3–25) |
| **Industry** | SaaS, fintech, marketplace, healthcare analytics, e-commerce |
| **Warehouse** | Snowflake **or** BigQuery (100+ tables minimum) |
| **Transformation layer** | dbt in production (any version) |
| **VCS** | GitHub or GitLab |
| **Pain signal** | At least 2–3 schema-related incidents per quarter |

## Buyer Personas

| Persona | Title Examples | Why they care |
|---------|---------------|--------------|
| **Buyer** | Head of Data Platform, Director of Data Engineering | Owns MTTD, MTTR, schema change policy |
| **Champion** | Senior Data Engineer, Analytics Engineer | Owns dbt, gets paged when downstream breaks |
| **End user** | Data Engineer, Analytics Engineer | Wants velocity without breakage |

## Sourcing Playbook

1. **LinkedIn Boolean Search:**
   - `(dbt OR Snowflake OR BigQuery) AND ("Head of Data" OR "Director of Data Engineering")`
   - Filter by company headcount 50–1,000.
2. **dbt Slack Community:**
   - Watch `#showcase` for dbt adoption announcements.
3. **DataHub / OpenMetadata Contributors:**
   - GitHub orgs with metadata connectors to dbt.
4. **Conference Talks:**
   - dbt Coalesce, Snowflake Summit, Big Data LDN.
5. **Personal Network:**
   - Reach out to data engineers you've worked with; ask for warm intros.
